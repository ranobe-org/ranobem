Manga Reader Pro is a fast, focused reader for manga, manhwa, manhua and webtoons. Build your library, get a notification when a new chapter is out, and read in the layout that suits the series: long webtoon strips or classic right to left pages.


📖 A READER FOR EVERY KIND OF COMIC
• Webtoon mode: one continuous vertical scroll for manhwa and long strips
• Right to left for Japanese manga, left to right, or vertical pages
• Smart fit, fit width or fit screen, with side padding and page gap controls
• Black, gray or white background, fullscreen, keep screen on, brightness and orientation lock
• Turn pages by tapping the screen edges or with the volume keys
• The next chapter loads on its own when you reach the end

🔔 NEW CHAPTER ALERTS
• Your library is checked every 12 hours, and a notification tells you which series updated and which chapter is out
• Tap "Check now" whenever you can't wait
• New chapters are tagged in the chapter list

✅ ALWAYS KNOW WHERE YOU STOPPED
• Read chapters are checked off and your last read chapter is marked
• Reading history takes you back to any series in one tap
• Search a chapter list or flip its order

🔄 ANILIST SYNC
• Link a series to your AniList account and your progress, status, score, dates and notes update as you read
• Chapters you read on AniList or in another app show up as read here
• Offline changes are sent when you're back online

📥 READ OFFLINE
• Save a whole series or a few chapters as EPUB books
• Open them in any e-book reader, share them, or keep them for a flight

📚 FIND YOUR NEXT SERIES
• Browse the latest updates, search by title or filter by genre
• Series pages show status, release year, followers, genres, authors, related series and recommendations

🌙 BUILT FOR LONG READING SESSIONS
• Light and dark themes, or follow your phone
• Small, quick to open, and no sign-up (AniList login is optional)
• In English and Russian

Questions or ideas? Find us on Discord, linked in the app's Settings.
