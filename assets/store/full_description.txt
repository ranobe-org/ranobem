Manga Reader Pro is a clean, fast reader for manga, manhwa, manhua and webtoons. Follow the series you love, see the moment a new chapter drops, and read it the way you like, with no ads.

Whether you binge long-running shonen, scroll through Korean webtoons or catch up on the latest manhua, everything lives in one simple library.


🔔 NEVER MISS A CHAPTER
• New chapter notifications: your library is checked every 12 hours and you're told which series updated and which chapter is out
• "Check now" looks for new chapters in your library whenever you want
• New chapters are tagged "New" in the chapter list, so you can spot them right away

📖 NEVER LOSE YOUR PLACE
• Chapters you've read are checked off
• Your last read chapter is always marked
• Search the chapter list or flip its order with one tap
• Your reading history lets you jump back in where you left off

📚 YOUR LIBRARY
• Save series to your library and keep everything you follow together
• Series pages show the status, release year, followers, genres, authors, related series and recommendations
• Search by title, filter by genre, or find more from the same author

🎨 READ IT YOUR WAY
• Four reading modes: webtoon scroll, left to right, right to left (classic manga) and vertical pages
• Fit screen, fit width or smart fit for long strips
• Adjust side padding and the gap between pages
• Black, gray or white background
• Tap the screen edges or use the volume keys to turn pages
• Fullscreen mode, keep screen on, custom brightness and orientation lock
• The next chapter loads automatically when you reach the end

📥 TAKE IT OFFLINE
• Save a whole series or just a few chapters as EPUB books
• Open or share them from the downloads screen and read in any e-book reader, even without internet

🌙 LIGHT OR DARK
• Light and dark themes, or follow your phone's setting
• Made for late-night reading sessions

⚡ LIGHT AND FAST
• Small app that opens quickly
• No ads, no sign-up, no account needed
• Available in English and Russian

Genres include action, adventure, romance, fantasy, isekai, comedy, drama, horror, mystery, sci-fi, slice of life, sports, school life, martial arts and more.

Every feature is unlocked: new chapter notifications, EPUB downloads and the full reader, with no ads. Thank you for supporting development of the app.
